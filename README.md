# Demo Café Group: Audit Analytics in Excel

This project investigates changes in financial performance and financial position between 2024 and 2025, alongside branch and monthly sales performance during 2025.

It brings financial statements, expense schedules, general-ledger evidence and management explanations into a single Excel analytical review.

## Project files

- [Download the Excel workbook](Demo_Cafe_Audit_Analysis.xlsx)

Download the workbook and open it in Microsoft Excel to explore the five worksheets and their formulas.

## Questions explored

- What drove changes in revenue, operating profit and the annual loss?
- Which expense categories explain the largest changes in costs?
- How did assets, liabilities and equity change between the two years?
- Which branches contributed most to annual sales?
- Which months performed best, and how did sales change from the previous month?

## Key findings

| Finding | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue growth | Revenue rose from KSh 4.053 billion to KSh 4.346 billion, an increase of 7.2%. Beverage sales contributed about 71% of the increase. |
| Gross margin | Gross margin improved from 50.0% to 52.4%, as revenue increased faster than cost of sales. |
| Operating performance | Operating profit increased by 50.0% to KSh 239.9 million. The annual loss narrowed from KSh 117.5 million to KSh 48.5 million. |
| Property, plant and equipment | Additions of KSh 194.1 million were offset by depreciation and net disposals. Net PPE decreased from KSh 630.0 million to KSh 584.9 million. |
| Branch concentration | Branch 1 generated KSh 193.6 million, or 4.5% of company sales. The top five branches contributed 19.4%. |
| Monthly sales | December ranked first at KSh 434.2 million, accounting for 10.0% of annual sales and increasing 17.8% from November. February had the lowest monthly total. |

## Workbook structure

| Worksheet | Analysis |
| --- | --- |
| Profit and Loss | 2024–2025 comparison, account breakdowns, absolute and percentage variances, and analytical remarks. |
| Balance Sheet | Changes in assets, liabilities and equity, with supporting explanations. |
| Schedule of Expenses | Expense comparisons and commentary on the main cost drivers. |
| Branch Sales | Monthly sales and monthly contribution percentages, annual totals and annual contribution percentages, sorted by annual sales. |
| Monthly Sales Ranking | Months ranked by total sales, share of annual sales, month-on-month changes and remarks. |

## Approach and calculations

1. Compare the final financial-statement amounts for 2024 and 2025.
2. Calculate each change as **2025 minus 2024**. Percentage variance uses the absolute 2024 amount as the denominator. A zero 2024 base leaves the percentage blank.
3. Use supporting schedules, 2025 ledger evidence and management explanations to explain significant movements.
4. Rank branches by annual sales and calculate each branch's share of both monthly and annual company sales.
5. Rank months by sales and calculate month-on-month changes against the preceding calendar month, regardless of the displayed ranking order.

## Scope and interpretation

- The three financial-review sheets use **KSh thousands**. The two sales sheets use **full KSh**.
- Company sales include special orders. Special orders, head office and central production are shown separately from the branch ranking.
- Branch analysis measures **sales**, rather than branch profitability. Branch-level cost allocations are not included.
- Seasonal and operational explanations draw on management commentary and observed sales patterns. They do not, on their own, establish causation.
- The workbook is a portfolio analytical review and does not provide an audit opinion.

## Data presentation

**Demo Café Group** is a fictional company name. Outlet names are replaced with consistent labels such as **Branch 1** and **Branch 2**. These labels do not identify actual locations. Financial figures have not been converted to synthetic data. Raw ledgers and client workpapers are excluded from this repository.
